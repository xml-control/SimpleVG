# Facilities & Building Presets

The **FACILITIES** library section contains 12 architectural structure presets for plant site layouts, campus maps, and building automation diagrams.

> [!TIP]
> **Source of Inspiration**: All facilities presets are provided as a **source of inspiration** to help you quickly build site overviews!

## Available Presets

1. **Compass Rose**: Traditional N-S-E-W compass dial with intermediate bearings and bold North indicator arrow.
2. **House**: 2-Storey residential building with gabled roof and entry door.
3. **Bungalow**: Single-storey building structure with windows.
4. **Garage**: Workshop/garage building with sectional roll-up door.
5. **Workshop**: Industrial gabled workshop hall.
6. **Greenhouse**: Glass panel gabled greenhouse enclosure.
7. **Office**: Commercial office building with multi-story window grid.
8. **Tower Block**: Dark gray multi-storey high rise tower block.
9. **Factory**: Industrial factory complex with sawtooth roof structure.
10. **New Shop**: Modern retail storefront with large display windows.
11. **Vintage Shop**: Double-fronted traditional shop front with awnings.
12. **American Diner**: Retro stainless-steel diner building.
