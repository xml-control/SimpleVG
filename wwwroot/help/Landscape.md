# Landscape & Environment Presets

The **LANDSCAPE** section contains 10 environment and outdoor feature presets for environmental Monitoring, site schematics, and facility surrounds.

> [!TIP]
> **Source of Inspiration**: Provided to inspire site environment detailing!

## Available Presets

1. **Tree**: Deciduous tree with wooden trunk, multi-toned lush leaf canopy lobes, and ground shadow.
2. **Pine Tree**: Layered conifer evergreen tree with 3 stacked pine tiers.
3. **Shrub**: Rounded bush cluster with leaf highlights and ground shadow.
4. **Flower Bed**: Soil garden bed with 5 colorful blooming flowers (red, violet, yellow, pink, orange) and green leaves.
5. **Fence**: Wooden picket fence panel with pointed slats and horizontal support rails.
6. **Trellis**: Wooden lattice garden trellis frame with climbing green vines and flower buds.
7. **Brick Wall**: Running bond brick wall section with mortar joints and coping stone cap.
8. **Pond**: Organic curved water pond with ripples, floating lily pads, and a pink lotus flower blossom.
9. **Block Paving**: Interlocking paver patio section with alternating terracotta and slate block pavers.
10. **Hedge**: Manicured garden topiary hedge with leafy highlights.
