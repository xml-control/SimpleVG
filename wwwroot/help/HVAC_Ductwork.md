# HVAC & Ductwork Presets

The **HVAC & DUCTWORK** library section contains 10 ductwork and air handling unit presets for building management system (BMS) schematics.

> [!TIP]
> **Source of Inspiration**: Provided to help get you started designing custom ventilation, airflow, and environmental control schematics!

## Available Presets

1. **Straight Duct**: Rectangular duct segment with connecting end flanges.
2. **Duct Elbow**: 90° smooth duct bend with internal airflow turning vanes and connecting flanges.
3. **Duct Tee**: 3-Way T-Junction rectangular duct fitting with connecting flange ribs on all 3 ports.
4. **Duct Reducer**: Concentric duct reducer transition element.
5. **Air Damper**: Airflow louver damper with adjustable blade angles.
6. **AHU Unit**: Modular Air Handling Unit housing cabinet.
7. **Air Filter**: Pleated cell air filter.
8. **Heater Coil**: Serpentine electric/hot-water heating coil assembly.
9. **Cooler Coil**: Chilled water cooling coil with condensate drip tray.
10. **Bag Filter**: High-efficiency pocket/bag filter bank assembly.
