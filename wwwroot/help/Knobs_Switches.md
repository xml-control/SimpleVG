# Knobs & Switches Presets

The **KNOBS & SWITCHES** section provides 6 interactive control presets for panel layouts.

## Available Presets

1. **Rotary Knob**: Analog rotary dial with radial position pointer indicator.
2. **Push Button**: Panel-mount push button switch.
3. **Toggle Switch**: 2-position industrial toggle switch.
4. **Selector Switch**: 3-position rotary selector switch (`HAND - OFF - AUTO`).
5. **E-Stop Button**: Emergency Stop mushroom push button with yellow safety legend plate.
6. **Key Switch**: Lockable key-operated control switch.
