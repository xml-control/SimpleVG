# Sensors & Instrumentation Presets

The **SENSORS** library section contains 8 sensor presets split into ductwork, room wall stat, and outdoor weather sensor styles.

## Available Presets

### Ductwork Sensors (Round Body with Probe Stem)
1. **Duct Temp**: Duct insertion temperature sensor probe with round terminal head and duct flange.
2. **Duct Humidity**: Duct humidity transducer with protective filter tip.
3. **Duct Pressure**: Duct differential pressure transducer with high/low pressure port nipples.

### Room Wall Sensors (Square Wall Housing with Air Slots)
4. **Room Temp**: Square wall-mounted room thermostat with air ventilation slots.
5. **Room Humidity**: Room humidity sensor with dual airflow slots.

### Outdoor Weather Sensors
6. **Outside Temp**: Outdoor ambient temperature sensor with multi-plate solar radiation shield.
7. **Outside Humidity**: Weatherproof outdoor relative humidity sensor probe.
8. **Outside UV**: Outdoor solar UV radiation sensor dome.
