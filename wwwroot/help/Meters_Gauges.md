# Meters & Gauges Presets

The **METERS & GAUGES** section provides 5 instrumentation measurement presets.

## Available Presets

1. **Pressure Gauge**: Analog dial pressure gauge with dial markings, needle pointer, and central hub.
2. **Level/Temp Bar**: Vertical bargraph thermometer/level indicator.
3. **Digital Meter**: Digital 7-segment LED panel readout meter.
4. **Flowmeter**: Inline liquid flowmeter rotor body with flange connectors.
5. **Watt Meter**: Dial power/wattage meter with central pointer.
