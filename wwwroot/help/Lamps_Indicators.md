# Lamps & Indicators Presets

The **LAMPS & INDICATORS** section provides 9 pilot light, signal tower, and alarm annunciator presets.

## Available Presets

1. **Green Pilot Light**: Traditional pilot lamp with metallic bezel ring, translucent green lens, glass reflection curve, and inner filament cross (`X`) symbol (*Normal / Running*).
2. **Red Alarm Light**: Industrial red pilot light with metallic bezel and inner filament cross (*Fault / Trip / Stop*).
3. **Amber Warning Light**: Amber pilot light (*Caution / Alert / Pre-alarm*).
4. **Blue Pilot Light**: Sapphire blue pilot light (*Ready / Auto / Manual*).
5. **Alarm Beacon**: Industrial strobe dome alarm beacon with fresnel glass ridges, metallic base, and radiating flash glow rays.
6. **3-Tier Stack Light**: Vertical 3-tier industrial signal tower column (Red top, Amber middle, Green bottom) with black mounting pole and base pedestal.
7. **Flush LED Panel**: Modern flush-mount LED panel indicator with status bar and `RUN` readout.
8. **Annunciator Tile**: Traditional backlit industrial alarm annunciator window tile with alarm header and subtitle (`ALARM TRIP / HIGH TEMP`).
9. **Dome Pilot Light**: Raised dome lens pilot light on a threaded panel mount socket.
