# Motors & Pumps Presets

The **MOTORS & PUMPS** section contains 4 rotating machinery presets.

## Available Presets

1. **Pump**: Horizontal centrifugal pump with spiral casing, suction nozzle, discharge nozzle, and mounting feet.
2. **AC Motor**: Industrial AC electric motor with cooling fin ribs and shaft coupler.
3. **Agitator**: Vertical tank mixer / agitator assembly with drive motor and impeller blades.
4. **Blower Fan**: Centrifugal ventilation blower fan assembly.
