# Pipes & Connectors Presets

The **PIPES & CONNECTORS** section provides 4 3D piping and fitting primitives.

## Available Presets

1. **3D Pipe**: Straight cylindrical metal pipe segment with 3D gradient highlight.
2. **Pipe Elbow**: 90° pipe elbow bend with inner turning radius and connection flanges.
3. **Pipe Tee**: 3-way T-junction pipe fitting.
4. **Flange**: Bolted pipe flange coupling joint.
