# Welcome to SimpleVG

**SimpleVG** is a modern, lightweight vector graphic editor and SCADA HMI diagram creation suite built directly in your browser.

> [!TIP]
> **Source of Inspiration**: All pre-built SCADA preset symbols in SimpleVG (Pipes, Motors, Pumps, HVAC, Sensors, Facilities, Landscape, etc.) are provided as a **source of inspiration** to help get you started quickly! Feel free to drop them onto your canvas, inspect their styling, ungroup, resize, rotate, and combine them to build your custom operational graphics.

---

## Key Features

- **Rich Vector Drawing Tools**: Rectangles, Circles, Polylines, Polygons, Stars, Arrows, Arcs, Bézier Pen, Freehand paths, and Text.
- **11 Pre-Built SCADA Symbol Libraries**: Sorted alphabetically for instant access.
- **ISA-101 Gray HMI Mode**: Toggle high-performance ISA-101 compliant gray palettes with one click.
- **Precision Alignment & Grid Snapping**: Built-in 20px grid alignment, snapping toggle, and pixel-perfect keyboard nudging (Arrow keys).
- **Ruler View**: Toggleable top and left canvas pixel rulers.
- **Clean File Export**: Save and open native SVG files, or export high-resolution PNG images.
